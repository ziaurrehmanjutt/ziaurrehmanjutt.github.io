In short: Diya Expense works completely offline. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

1. Information you enter in the app

The app lets you record accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, loans, invoices, installments, recurring payments, notes, tags and receipt photos. This information is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to any server, and we have no access to it.

2. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-up or login is needed.
  • The app does not connect to the internet or to any server.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools are included.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.

3. Permissions the app may ask for

NotificationsTo show reminders for upcoming payments. Reminders are created on your device.
Exact alarmsTo deliver a reminder at the time you chose.
Run at startupTo restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
BiometricsTo unlock the app with your fingerprint or face. Your biometric data is handled by Android and is never available to the app or to us.
Photos / cameraOnly when you choose to attach a receipt photo. You pick the picture through the system picker. The photo is resized and stored inside the app on your device.

4. App lock

If you set a PIN, only a salted, one-way hash of it is stored on your device. We cannot see or recover your PIN.

5. Backup, export and sharing

You can export a backup file (JSON) or a CSV spreadsheet of your transactions. These files are created on your device and you decide where they go, using the Android share sheet or a download. They are not encrypted, so please keep them safe. Importing reads only the file you select.

6. Device backups by Android

If you have turned on Android's system backup, your phone's operating system may include app data in your Google backup. That is controlled by your device settings and by Google, not by this app. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything at any time from More → Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Children

The app is a general finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email ziaa520@gmail.com.
