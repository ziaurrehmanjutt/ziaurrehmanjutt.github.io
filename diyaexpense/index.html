Features

Diya Expense is a private money manager that works completely offline. Record spending in seconds, follow your budget and savings goals, keep track of what you owe and what you are owed, and see clearly where your money goes.

✏️

Track everything

Expenses, income and transfers in seconds. Unlimited accounts, your own categories, #tags and receipt photos.

🎯

Budgets and savings goals

Set a monthly budget per category and follow it on your home screen. Create goals with a target and add funds as you go.

📊

Clear insights

A spending donut by category, a 6-month income vs. expense trend and a month-end forecast.

🤝

Loans, invoices and installments

Track money you gave or took, invoices you issued or received, and split purchases into scheduled installments.

🔔

Recurring payments and reminders

Rent, bills, subscriptions and salary on autopilot, with local notifications before they are due.

🔒

Private and secure

Everything stays on your device. PIN and fingerprint or face lock. No tracking and no internet needed.

💾

Backup and export

Back up to a file and restore on a new phone, or export every transaction to CSV for Excel and Google Sheets.

🌍

Made for everyone

English, Arabic, Urdu, French and Spanish with full right-to-left support, plus light and dark themes.

FAQ

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no sign-up and the app works completely offline.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. The app does not send your data to any server. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I move to a new phone?

Back up your data to a file in Settings, copy it to your new phone, and import it there. You can also export transactions as CSV.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Arabic, Urdu, French and Spanish, with right-to-left layouts and light and dark themes.

Can I track loans and installments?

Yes. Track money you gave or took, invoices and installments, with partial payments and an overall net position.
