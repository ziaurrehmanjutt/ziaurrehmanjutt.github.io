Features

DiyaQR is a free QR code scanner and QR code generator in one fast, beautiful app. Scan any QR code or barcode with your camera or from a picture and act on it in one tap. Create your own QR code for a link, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, contact card, event or location, style it with your colors and logo, then save or share it in seconds. No account, no sign-up, no watermark.

FAQ

Is DiyaQR free?

Yes. DiyaQR is free to download and use. The app shows ads, and there is no account, subscription or sign-up.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account. Creating, styling, saving and scanning QR codes all work on your phone without an account, and your codes and scans stay on your device.

What kinds of QR codes can I make?

Links, text, email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi, contact cards, calendar events and map locations. You can add a logo, colors, gradients and custom dot and corner shapes.

Will my styled QR code still scan?

DiyaQR warns you when a design may be hard to scan, for example low contrast or a large logo on a low error-correction level. Print a test copy before you print many.

Can I make QR codes without installing the app?

Yes. Use the free online QR generator on this site. It runs in your browser, adds no watermark and does not upload your data.

Which formats can I export?

PNG, JPEG, SVG and WebP. SVG is best for printing at any size.

Which languages does DiyaQR support?

English, Arabic, Spanish, French, Hindi, Urdu, Indonesian, Portuguese, Turkish, Bengali, Filipino and Persian, with right-to-left layouts.
