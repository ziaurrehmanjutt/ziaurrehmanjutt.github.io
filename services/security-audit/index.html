Overview

What this is

I review your website or web application against the OWASP Top 10, the industry standard list of the most serious web risks. You receive a plain-language report that explains each finding, how serious it is, and exactly how to fix it. I can also apply the fixes.

Who it's for: Owners of websites that handle logins, payments or customer data, companies preparing for a client or compliance review, and anyone who suspects their site was hacked or is running old software.

Scope

What you get

  • Check against the OWASP Top 10: broken access control, injection (SQL and others), XSS, insecure design, security misconfiguration, vulnerable and outdated components, authentication failures, integrity failures, logging gaps, SSRF
  • Review of login, session, password reset and permission logic
  • Server and HTTPS checks: SSL/TLS setup, security headers, exposed files, open admin panels
  • Dependency and plugin review for known vulnerabilities
  • Written report: each finding with severity, evidence, and a concrete fix
  • Optional: I implement the fixes and re-test to confirm they are closed
Process

How it works

1

Agree scope

We confirm exactly which site, app or API I may test. I only test systems you own or are authorized to give me access to.

2

Test

Manual review and tooling against the OWASP Top 10, careful not to disrupt your live site.

3

Report

You get a prioritized report in plain language, with fixes, not just a list of tool output.

4

Fix & re-test

Optionally I apply the fixes and re-test so you know the issues are really closed.

Questions

FAQ

Is it safe for my live website?

Yes. I avoid destructive tests on production, and for riskier checks I suggest a staging copy.

Do you guarantee my site can't be hacked?

No one can. An audit greatly reduces risk by closing known weaknesses, but security is ongoing work.

Will you test any website I name?

No. I only test sites you own or have written permission to test.
