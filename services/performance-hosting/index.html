Overview

What this is

Slow or unstable sites lose customers and rankings. I set up and tune servers, deploy your application properly, and make sure SSL, backups and monitoring are in place.

Who it's for: Owners of slow websites, businesses moving hosts, and teams that need a clean deployment setup.

Scope

What you get

  • Speed audit and optimization: caching, compression, images, database queries
  • Server setup and hardening on Linux, Windows/IIS, cPanel and Plesk
  • Cloud hosting on Azure, AWS and GCP
  • SSL/TLS certificates and security headers
  • Automated backups and a tested restore process
  • Deployment pipelines and website or server migration with minimal downtime

Third-party costs (hosting, domains, app store fees, API or AI usage, paid plugins) are paid by you directly to those providers and are not part of my fee.
