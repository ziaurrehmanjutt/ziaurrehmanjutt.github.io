Overview

What this is

Good content is wasted if search engines can't crawl, understand or trust your site. I fix the technical foundations first (indexing, speed, structure, schema), then improve pages so they match what people actually search for.

Who it's for: Business owners, shops, clinics, agencies and app makers whose website is live but gets little or no search traffic, or whose rankings dropped.

Scope

What you get

  • Full technical SEO audit: crawlability, indexing, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical tags, redirects, broken links
  • On-page optimization: titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, image alt text
  • Structured data (schema.org) for rich results: organization, product, FAQ, software app, article
  • Speed and Core Web Vitals improvements: image optimization, caching, script cleanup
  • Multilingual SEO with hreflang for sites in several languages (Arabic, Urdu, English and more)
  • Google Search Console and Analytics setup, with a plain-language report of what changed

Questions

FAQ

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes can show effects within weeks, but ranking growth usually takes 2 to 6 months depending on competition. I can't promise a position on Google, and anyone who does is not being honest.

Do you do link building?

I focus on technical and on-page SEO and on content structure. I don't sell spammy links, which can get a site penalized.

Can you work on my WordPress or custom site?

Yes. I work with WordPress, Laravel, PHP, Angular and static sites.
