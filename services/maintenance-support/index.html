Overview

What this is

Websites and apps need regular care: software updates, security patches, store compliance changes and the occasional bug. I take that off your plate under a simple support arrangement.

Who it's for: Owners of existing websites and apps, including ones built by someone else, who want a reliable developer on call.

Scope

What you get

  • Bug fixes and small improvements
  • Framework, plugin and dependency updates
  • Security patches and backups
  • Mobile app updates for new Android and iOS requirements and store re-submissions
  • Uptime and error monitoring
  • Priority response when something breaks
