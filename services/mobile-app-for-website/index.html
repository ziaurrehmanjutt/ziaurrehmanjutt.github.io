Overview

What this is

I turn your existing website or web application into a real Android and iOS app using Ionic, Angular and Capacitor. I have published 10+ Android and 5+ iOS apps and managed store accounts for clients, so I handle the full path from code to Play Store and App Store.

Who it's for: Businesses with a website, store or portal that want an app for engagement, push notifications, or because customers expect one.

Scope

What you get

  • Android and iOS app from one codebase (Ionic, Angular, Capacitor)
  • Native features: push notifications, camera, QR scanner, location, offline storage, deep links
  • Login and API connection to your existing website or backend
  • App icon, splash screen and store listing assets
  • Publishing: Google Play Console and Apple App Store Connect setup and submission
  • Updates and support after launch

What affects the cost

  • Wrapper of your site versus a properly designed app with native screens
  • Number of screens and features (push, offline, payments, maps)
  • Whether a backend or API already exists or must be built
  • Android only or Android and iOS
  • Store fees (Google Play one-time fee, Apple yearly developer fee) are paid by you to the stores
Third-party costs (hosting, domains, app store fees, API or AI usage, paid plugins) are paid by you directly to those providers and are not part of my fee.
Questions

FAQ

Will Apple accept a simple website wrapper?

Apple requires apps to offer more than a plain website. I add real app features such as notifications, offline use or native screens to meet the guidelines. Approval is Apple's decision.

Who owns the app and store accounts?

You do. I can set up the accounts in your name.

Can you update my existing app?

Yes, I can take over and improve existing Ionic and Angular apps.
